## What is TAE Phase D?

TAE (Team Alignment Engine) Phase D brings **organizational intelligence** to your team's decision-making:

### The 6 Capabilities You'll Use:

**D1: Portfolio Analytics** 📊
- See all your team's decisions in one place
- Track decision health and identify bottlenecks
- Get strategic insights powered by AI

**D2: Real-Time Collaboration** 🤝
- See who's actively working on decisions
- Get instant updates when teammates take actions
- Collaborate synchronously or asynchronously

**D3: Decision Dependencies** 🔗
- Link related decisions together
- Visualize how decisions impact each other
- Prevent conflicts and circular dependencies

**D4: Organizational Patterns** 🧠
- Learn from past decisions
- Identify what works and what doesn't
- Get recommendations based on team history

**D5: Advanced Analytics** 📈
- Track decision-making trends over time
- Forecast decision timelines
- Benchmark against best practices

**D6: Cross-Team Coordination** 🌐
- Coordinate decisions across multiple teams
- Detect conflicts early
- Align on shared goals

## Your First Decision

Let's walk through creating and completing your first decision in TAE:

### Step 1: Create a New Decision

1. Click **"+ New Decision"** in top right
2. Fill in decision details:
   - **Topic:** "Choose Q1 2025 OKRs" (example)
   - **Type:** Feature prioritization, pricing, hiring, etc.
   - **Context:** Why this decision matters
   - **Stakeholders:** Add team members
   - **Alignment Mode:** Evidence-backed (recommended for pilot)

3. Click **"Create Session"**

### Step 2: Add Perspectives

Each stakeholder should add their perspective:

1. Navigate to **"Perspectives"** tab
2. Click **"Add My Perspective"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Goals:** What you want to achieve (rank priorities)
   - **Concerns:** What worries you
   - **Constraints:** Any limitations or requirements

**Why this matters:** TAE uses perspectives to identify disagreements and align stakeholders.

### Step 3: Propose Options

Brainstorm possible solutions:

1. Go to **"Options"** tab
2. Click **"+ Propose Option"**
3. Describe the option:
   - **Name:** "Option A: Focus on Enterprise Features"
   - **Description:** What this option entails
   - **Assumptions:** What needs to be true
   - **Trade-offs:** What you're giving up

4. **Pro Tip:** Use **"AI Suggest"** (Phase C) for AI-generated options

### Step 4: Validate with CEE (Optional)

For critical decisions, validate assumptions:

1. Select an option
2. Click **"Validate Assumptions"** (Phase C)
3. CEE will:
   - Identify causal claims in your assumptions
   - Suggest evidence to test them
   - Recommend experiments

### Step 5: Vote and Converge

After discussion, stakeholders vote:

1. Go to **"Voting"** tab
2. Rate each option:
   - **Strong Support** (green)
   - **Mild Support** (light green)
   - **Neutral** (gray)
   - **Mild Concern** (light red)
   - **Strong Concern** (red)

3. TAE calculates:
   - **Fit scores** - How well option aligns with goals
   - **Disagreements** - Where stakeholders differ
   - **Minority concerns** - Important dissenting views

### Step 6: Reach Alignment

When ready:

1. Click **"Move to Alignment"**
2. TAE shows:
   - Recommended option (highest fit + alignment)
   - Key disagreements to resolve
   - Minority concerns to address

3. Discussion continues until consensus

### Step 7: Make Decision

Final step:

1. Click **"Finalize Decision"**
2. Document:
   - **Chosen option:** What you decided
   - **Rationale:** Why you chose it
   - **Next actions:** What happens next

3. Decision moves to **"Complete"** status

### Step 8: Retrospective (Phase C)

After implementing the decision:

1. Go to **"Retrospective"** tab
2. Answer:
   - How did it go?
   - What would you do differently?
   - Quality rating (1-10)

**This feeds Phase D4 (Organizational Patterns) for future learning!**

## Phase D Capabilities

### D1: Portfolio Analytics

**Access:** Dashboard → Portfolio

**What you'll see:**
- All your organization's decisions
- Health scores by team/type
- Bottlenecks and stuck sessions
- Strategic insights from AI

**Common Use Cases:**
- **Weekly team review:** Check decision health
- **Monthly planning:** Identify patterns and themes
- **Leadership reporting:** Show decision-making velocity

**Example Workflow:**
```
1. Navigate to Portfolio → Analytics
2. Set date range (last 30 days)
3. Filter by your team
4. Review:
   - Health Score (aim for >0.8)
   - Stuck sessions (follow up)
   - Clusters (related decisions)
   - Strategic insights (AI recommendations)
```

### D2: Real-Time Collaboration

**Access:** Active in all decision sessions

**What you'll see:**
- 🟢 Green dots - Teammates online
- 👀 Eyes - Who's viewing same section
- ⌨️ Typing indicator - Someone is editing

**Features:**
- **Presence Tracking** - See who's active
- **Action Broadcasting** - Real-time updates
- **Heartbeat** - Auto-sync every 30s

**Example Workflow:**
```
1. Open a decision session
2. See who else is online (top right)
3. Watch updates appear live:
   - "Alice proposed Option C"
   - "Bob voted on Option A"
   - "Carol raised a concern"
4. Your actions broadcast immediately
```

### D3: Decision Dependencies

**Access:** Dashboard → Dependencies or any session

**What you'll see:**
- **Graph view:** Visual dependency map
- **List view:** Dependencies by type
- **Conflicts:** Circular dependencies detected

**Dependency Types:**
- **Blocks:** "A must complete before B"
- **Depends On:** "A requires B's outcome"
- **Relates To:** "A and B are connected"
- **Supersedes:** "A replaces B"

**Example Workflow:**
```
1. In a decision session, go to Dependencies tab
2. Click "+ Add Dependency"
3. Select related decision
4. Choose type: "Blocks" (this blocks other decision)
5. Add description: "Q1 OKRs must be set before hiring plan"
6. Save - TAE checks for circular dependencies
```

**Common Use Cases:**
- **Planning sprints:** Link feature decisions
- **Resource allocation:** Show competing priorities
- **Risk management:** Identify critical paths

### D4: Organizational Patterns

**Access:** Dashboard → Patterns

**What you'll see:**
- **Success Patterns:** What works for your team
- **Failure Indicators:** Warning signs
- **Recommendations:** AI-powered suggestions

**Example Insights:**
- "Decisions with >5 stakeholders take 2x longer"
- "Feature prioritization decisions succeed when PMreviews early"
- "Pricing decisions benefit from customer validation first"

**Example Workflow:**
```
1. Navigate to Patterns
2. Set lookback period (90 days recommended)
3. Review:
   - Success patterns (green)
   - Failure indicators (red)
   - Recommendations (blue)
4. Apply learnings to current decisions
```

### D5: Advanced Analytics

**Access:** Dashboard → Analytics

**Features:**
- **Trend Analysis:** Decision time trends
- **Forecasting:** Predict decision timelines
- **Benchmarking:** Compare to baselines

**Example Workflow:**
```
1. Navigate to Analytics → Trends
2. Select metric: "Decision Time"
3. View trend over last 90 days
4. See forecast for next 30 days
5. Compare to organizational benchmark
```

**Common Use Cases:**
- **Process improvement:** Track velocity over time
- **Capacity planning:** Forecast team bandwidth
- **Goal setting:** Set realistic targets

### D6: Cross-Team Coordination

**Access:** Dashboard → Coordination

**What you'll see:**
- **Coordination Groups:** Multi-team decisions
- **Conflicts:** Resource/timeline/scope conflicts
- **Recommendations:** How to resolve

**Conflict Types:**
- **Temporal:** Overlapping timelines
- **Resource:** Same stakeholders/budget
- **Dependency:** Excessive dependency chains
- **Scope:** Overlapping boundaries

**Example Workflow:**
```
1. Create coordination group for related decisions
2. Add decisions from multiple teams
3. Click "Detect Conflicts"
4. Review detected conflicts:
   - "Engineering Team A and B both need Alice 20hrs/week"
   - "Product Launch conflicts with Infrastructure Migration timeline"
5. Resolve conflicts:
   - Adjust timelines
   - Reallocate resources
   - Merge related decisions
```

## Best Practices

### For Individual Stakeholders

✅ **DO:**
- Update your perspective early
- Vote thoughtfully with explanation
- Raise concerns specifically
- Review retrospectives
- Participate in real-time when possible

❌ **DON'T:**
- Vote without reading options
- Ignore minority concerns
- Skip retrospectives (they feed D4 learning!)
- Create duplicate dependencies

### For Decision Owners

✅ **DO:**
- Add all relevant stakeholders
- Set clear decision topic and context
- Use dependency tracking (D3)
- Check portfolio health (D1) weekly
- Document learnings in retrospectives

❌ **DON'T:**
- Skip CEE validation for critical decisions
- Ignore dependency conflicts
- Rush to decision without alignment
- Forget to update status

### For Teams

✅ **DO:**
- Review portfolio analytics (D1) in weekly team meetings
- Use patterns (D4) to inform process improvements
- Coordinate cross-team decisions (D6)
- Celebrate decisions that complete quickly
- Learn from decisions that struggle

❌ **DON'T:**
- Have too many concurrent decisions (>5 active per team)
- Ignore bottlenecks in portfolio view
- Make decisions in silos (use D6!)
- Forget to mark decisions complete

## Providing Feedback

### Your Feedback is CRITICAL!

As a pilot participant, your feedback directly shapes TAE's future. We need to know:

✅ **What's working well?**
✅ **What's confusing or frustrating?**
✅ **What features do you wish existed?**
✅ **How is TAE impacting your team?**

### Feedback Mechanisms

**1. Weekly Survey** (5 minutes, sent Fridays)
- Quick pulse check
- Rate your experience 1-10
- Open-ended feedback

**2. Bi-weekly Interview** (30 minutes, optional)
- Deep dive into your usage
- Screen share your workflows
- Collaborative improvement ideas

**3. In-App Feedback** (anytime)
- Click feedback button (bottom right)
- Report bugs or suggest features
- Include screenshots

**4. Team Retrospective** (monthly)
- Facilitated session with your whole team
- Discuss team-level insights
- Identify blockers and wins

### Success Metrics We're Tracking

We'll measure success by:

- **Decision velocity:** Time from start to completion (target: 2-5 days vs 2-3 weeks baseline)
- **Decision quality:** Retrospective ratings (target: >7/10 average)
- **Adoption:** % of team decisions using TAE (target: >80%)
- **Collaboration:** Real-time participation rate (target: >50% of decisions)
- **Satisfaction:** User satisfaction score (target: >8/10)

You'll see aggregated metrics in monthly pilot reports.

## FAQ

**Q: How much time will this take?**
A: TAE should save time, not add to it. Initial learning curve ~1-2 hours. After that, decisions should be faster.

**Q: What if TAE breaks during a critical decision?**
A: We have 24/7 support during pilot. You can always fall back to your normal process. Let us know immediately if this happens.

**Q: Will our data be private?**
A: Yes. All pilot data is organization-private. Aggregated anonymized metrics may be used for research.

**Q: What happens after the pilot?**
A: If successful, you'll transition to production TAE. Your data persists. If not successful, we work with you to improve.

**Q: Can we customize TAE for our team?**
A: Some customization via feature flags. Major customizations come in Phase E. Let us know what you need!

**Q: Do we have to use all Phase D features?**
A: No! Use what helps. We want to know which features you do/don't use and why.
