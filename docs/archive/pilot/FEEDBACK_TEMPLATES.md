## 2. Bi-Weekly Interview Guide

**Frequency:** Every 2 weeks (optional participation)
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Format:** Zoom video call with screen share

### Interview Script

**Introduction (2 minutes)**
```
Hi [Name], thanks for joining! This 30-minute interview will help us understand
how you're using TAE and where we can improve.

I'll ask about your experience, watch you use TAE, and gather improvement ideas.
Feel free to be brutally honest - we want to know what's NOT working!

Ready to dive in?
```

**Section 1: Usage Patterns (8 minutes)**

Q1: Walk me through how you used TAE this week.
- What decisions did you work on?
- Which Phase D features did you use?
- How did your team collaborate?

Q2: Which Phase D feature has been most valuable? Why?

Q3: Which Phase D feature has been least valuable? Why?

Q4: How does TAE fit into your team's workflow?
- When do you use it?
- Who's most/least engaged?
- Any friction points?

**Section 2: Live Demo (12 minutes)**

"Can you share your screen and show me how you use TAE?"

Watch for:
- ✅ Efficient workflows
- ❌ Confusion or frustration
- ⏱️ Time-consuming tasks
- 💡 Creative usage
- 🐛 Bugs or issues

Ask:
- "Why did you click that?"
- "What were you expecting to happen?"
- "How would you prefer this work?"

**Section 3: Impact & Improvements (8 minutes)**

Q5: How has TAE impacted your team's decision-making?
- Faster decisions?
- Better decisions?
- More alignment?
- Other benefits?

Q6: What's the biggest frustration with TAE right now?

Q7: If you could wave a magic wand and add one feature, what would it be?

Q8: Would you recommend TAE to another team? Why or why not?

**Wrap-Up (2 minutes)**

```
Thank you! Your feedback is invaluable. We'll incorporate your suggestions
into our roadmap.

Any final thoughts or questions?

[Thank and schedule next interview if desired]
```

- What positive changes did you notice?

**Questions to Answer:**

**Include:**
- Executive summary

## Feedback Etiquette

### For Pilot Teams

✅ **DO:**
- Be honest (good and bad!)
- Provide specific examples
- Suggest solutions, not just problems
- Respond to follow-up questions
- Share both individual and team perspectives

❌ **DON'T:**
- Hold back critique (we want to improve!)
- Provide vague feedback ("it's confusing")
- Only share negative feedback (tell us wins too!)
- Ignore surveys (we need your input!)

### For TAE Team

✅ **DO:**
- Thank teams for every piece of feedback
- Respond to critical issues within 2 hours
- Close the loop (share what we did with feedback)
- Be transparent about what we can/can't fix
- Celebrate pilot teams in final report

❌ **DON'T:**
- Get defensive about critiques
- Promise features we can't deliver
- Ignore "minor" feedback (patterns matter!)
- Delay responses to blocking issues

## Success Criteria

### Pilot Considered Successful If:

✅ **Adoption:** >80% of decisions use TAE

✅ **Velocity:** Decision time 2-5 days (vs 2-3 weeks baseline)

✅ **Quality:** Average retrospective rating >7/10

✅ **Satisfaction:** Average user satisfaction >8/10

✅ **Engagement:** >50% real-time collaboration participation

✅ **Recommendation:** >70% would recommend to other teams

### Pilot Outcome Scenarios:

**Scenario 1: Strong Success** (All criteria met)
- **Action:** Transition to production immediately
- **Next:** Expand to more teams, begin Phase E

**Scenario 2: Moderate Success** (Most criteria met)
- **Action:** Address top pain points, iterate 2-4 weeks
- **Next:** Re-assess, then transition to production

**Scenario 3: Mixed Results** (Some criteria met)
- **Action:** Deep dive on struggles, major improvements
- **Next:** Extended pilot or pivot approach

**Scenario 4: Unsuccessful** (Few criteria met)
- **Action:** Pause, reassess product-market fit
- **Next:** Major changes or pivot to different approach
